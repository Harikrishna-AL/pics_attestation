How it works

The attestation route, step by step.

Using UAE as an example: every country has its own chain, and we handle each stop for you.

  1. India

    MEA / MOFA

    Ministry of External Affairs attestation in your home country.

  2. India

    Embassy

    Attestation by the destination country's embassy in India.

  3. Destination

    MOFA

    Final attestation by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs abroad.

  4. Your door

    Scan & courier

    A scanned copy is shared first, then the originals are couriered back.
